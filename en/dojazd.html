Getting to Zatoka Cydrowa in Stegna

Address: ul. Grunwaldzka 12, 82-103 Stegna, Poland. By car from Gdańsk it is about 50 km (about 45 minutes) — the S7 expressway to the Nowy Dwór Gdański exit, then regional road 502 (about 14 km, about 15 minutes). Without a car, take bus line 870 from Gdańsk bus station. Free, monitored parking on site.

By car

From Gdańsk, the Tricity and southern Poland (S7 expressway)

  1. Take the S7 expressway towards Elbląg (from the south — towards Gdańsk).
  2. Leave at the Nowy Dwór Gdański junction.
  3. Follow regional road 502 through Tujsk and Rybina to Stegna (about 14 km, about 15 minutes).
  4. In Stegna head for ul. Grunwaldzka 12 — parking is on the property.

From Gdańsk city centre it is about 50 km and about 45 minutes. From Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport — about 1 hour.

Seasonal shortcut from Gdańsk: the Świbno–Mikoszewo ferry

From May to September you can also drive from Gdańsk along regional road 501 across Sobieszewo Island and take the ferry over the Vistula from Świbno to Mikoszewo, then continue through Jantar to Stegna. The ferry usually runs from 1 May to 30 September, 7:00–21:00, roughly every 30 minutes; the crossing is paid. In peak season there can be queues — then the S7 route is faster.

By bus from Gdańsk (line 870)

PKS Gdańsk line 870 runs from Gdańsk Bus Station (right next to Gdańsk Główny railway station) on the route Gdańsk – Jantar – Stegna – Sztutowo – Krynica Morska. The journey to Stegna takes about 1.5 hours. The bus stops at several places in Stegna (including Stegna, Dworzec). Timetables change seasonally — check the PKS Gdańsk website before you travel. Call us before arrival and we will tell you the best stop to get off.

By train

The easiest option is a train to Gdańsk Główny, then bus line 870 from the bus station next door (see above).

By plane

The nearest airport is Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport. The drive to Stegna takes about 1 hour (via the S7). By public transport: from the airport to Gdańsk Główny, then bus line 870.

By bike

The coastal R10 cycle route (EuroVelo 10) runs through Stegna — from Mikoszewo (the ferry from Świbno) it is about 11 km via Jantar. Read more about cycling routes on the Area page.

On site

  • Free, monitored parking on the property.
  • About 1500 m on foot through the forest to the beach, about 400 m to Stegna centre.
  • Pets are welcome.

Frequently asked questions about getting here

How do I drive to Stegna from Gdańsk?

Take the S7 to the Nowy Dwór Gdański exit, then road 502 to Stegna — about 50 km and about 45 minutes in total. Seasonally (May–September) you can also take road 501 and the Świbno–Mikoszewo ferry.

How do I get to Stegna without a car?

Take a train to Gdańsk Główny, then PKS Gdańsk bus line 870 from the bus station next door — the journey to Stegna takes about 1.5 hours.

Is there parking at Zatoka Cydrowa?

Yes, there is free, monitored parking for guests on the property.

How long is the drive from Gdańsk airport to Stegna?

About 1 hour by car, via the S7 and Nowy Dwór Gdański.

Questions about getting here?

Call or text us — we will suggest the easiest route and bus stop.
